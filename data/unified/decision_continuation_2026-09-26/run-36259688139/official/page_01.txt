Build a squad of 16 players within the 100M budget.

Squad Restrictions

Rule	Limit
Total Squad Size	16 Players
Budget	100M
Players Per Nation	Max 3

You can make unlimited transfers throughout the tournament, allowing you to react to team news and player form before every Gameweek.

TRANSFERS
Rule	Details
Transfers	Unlimited
Transfer Cost	None
Points Deduction	None
Transfer Deadline	Before the gameweek begins, unless you are a new user you can still make a squad past the deadline.
CAPTAIN & SUPER SUB

Captain

Rule	Effect
Captain	Scores 2x points

Choose one player from your Starting XV to be Captain each Gameweek. Your super sub is not eligible.

Super Sub

Scenario	Multiplier
Starts the Match	0.5x Points
Comes Off the Bench	3x Points
Does Not Play	0 Points

The Super Sub can be selected from any position but cannot also be your Captain.

BOOSTERS
Booster	Action
Co-captain	Choose a co-captain from your squad other than your super sub. For that gameweek, both your captain and vice captain score 2x points.
Triple Captain	For that matchday, the user's captain will score 3x points instead of 2x

HOW TO WIN
Strategy	Why It Matters
Pick In-Form Players	Maximise scoring opportunities
Monitor Team News	Avoid selecting non-starters
Choose the Right Captain	Double a player's score
Use Your Super Sub Wisely	Triple points from impact players
Make Regular Transfers	Adapt to fixtures and form

The manager with the highest overall score at the end of the tournament will finish top of the leaderboard.
