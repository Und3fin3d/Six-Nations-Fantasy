# Fantasy decision workflow protocol

Source base: `93f9670f38ed1699c0029192ac38227392b329fb`. Branch: `codex/fantasy-decision-workflow`.

The objective is a working and verified decision workflow with a fair incumbent comparison. Superiority is an empirical question. A negative result must retain the strongest operational implementation and all rejected candidates.

## Evidence already consulted

All PR #24 raw and official results, the 26 empirical/robust squads, five fixed interpolation weights, and the front-row scrum intervention have been inspected. Six Nations 2025 and 2026 and NCR 2026 GW1–3 are historical development/audit evidence. No part of them is labelled an untouched holdout. The original archives remain immutable.

## Decision measures

Realised total squad points are primary under identical pools, scoring, constraints and information cutoffs. Report each competition and season separately. Decompose XV, additional captain and full super-sub contributions. Missing selected outcomes make the total unknown. Preserve paired denominators and uncertainty.

Six Nations historical squads remain price-free diagnostics unless trustworthy lock-time prices and official positions can be recovered. NCR retains its recorded retrospective-input limitations. Do not turn an incomplete legal replay into a deployable-performance claim.

Forecast MAE, RMSE, mean bias, within-position ranks, feasible decision margins and regret under the same constraints are secondary. Record raw-event support, minutes/entry diagnostics, selected outcomes and influential-round sensitivity. Common scoring and coherent data contracts are mandatory even when correcting them reduces observed squad points.

## Chronological procedure

1. Repair supported scoring and availability contracts before comparing new candidates. Record scoring versions and input hashes. Reproduce archived predictions and decisions before interpreting changed outputs.
2. Include actual NCR and Six Nations deployed incumbents. Compare common-optimiser forecasts and each full deployed decision policy separately when their policies differ. Use exact shared candidate keys and causal feature cutoffs. Record any unsupported incumbent path or explicitly identified fallback.
3. Use Six Nations 2025 for initial chronological development. Apply a fixed recipe to Six Nations 2026 as a later historical audit. Outcomes have already been inspected, so this separation reduces new within-run tuning but does not restore independent confirmation.
4. For supervised forecast corrections, fit only on out-of-fold forecasts with outcomes available before the target lock. First-round forecasts use an unchanged declared fallback when no prior correction data exist. NCR has too few same-rubric rounds for an independent fitted calibration claim; use fixed recipes or strictly earlier rounds and disclose the denominator.
5. Admit a further remedy only when it addresses a recorded mechanism or failure. Register its formula, fitting window and comparator before running it. Retain every attempted remedy and revision, including failures. Do not choose parameters from the later audit and relabel it validation.

This table is a mechanism list, not an instruction to run every method. Source inspection and direct diagnostics determine which implementations are supported. Adding a materially different candidate requires an append-only registration explaining the observed failure it addresses.

## Advancement and stopping rules

A candidate can advance to a separately registered shadow when it improves mean historical squad points on its declared development measure, has a nonnegative later-audit total against the relevant comparator, and does not depend on a single round for the direction of its pooled advantage. Report any failed criterion; do not silently substitute a secondary metric. These criteria select research priorities and do not establish prospective superiority.

For a shared two-competition replacement, require a nonnegative difference in both competitions on comparable evidence. A competition-specific result can support only that route. Scoring repairs and immutable evidence capture can advance on correctness even if their historical totals decline.

If no new candidate meets these conditions, retain incumbent routing and keep the best supported candidate as a labelled shadow or research reference. Continue investigating materially distinct justified mechanisms and source defects. Stop a branch whose proposed mechanism is unsupported, whose data cannot identify the effect, or whose gain disappears on the declared audit. Do not relaunch it under another name without a changed hypothesis.

The protected `p3_event_50` NCR GW4–7 experiment remains unchanged. Preserve its original model, scoring semantics, history policy and declared severe-failure veto. Record correctness changes under explicit versions; never overwrite historical evidence. Any additional candidate has a separate protocol, immutable incumbent comparator, source/data/configuration hashes and predetermined evaluation point.

For a new prospective points comparison, use +5 points per round as the proposed minimum useful gain and −5 points per round as the proposed competition-specific deterioration margin. These are research decision thresholds, not measured benefits or a promise that four rounds can resolve them. A superiority claim requires the lower paired interval to exceed +5; a non-inferiority claim requires the lower bound to exceed −5. Report inconclusive evidence as inconclusive. These proposed thresholds do not replace the protected P3 veto.
